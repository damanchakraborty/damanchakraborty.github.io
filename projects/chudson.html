ChudSonGPT

A small language model built from scratch to understand the pieces behind modern language models.

PythonPyTorchCUDAIn progress

What it is

ChudSonGPT is an experimental LLM project. The goal is not to compete with production models, but to build and train a model while understanding every major component of the pipeline.

What I've worked on

  • Custom tokenization and vocabulary handling
  • PyTorch model training and validation
  • CUDA acceleration on NVIDIA hardware
  • Dataset streaming to avoid loading huge text files into RAM
  • Training checkpoints and validation-based model selection

Next

More experiments with architecture, data quality, training stability and inference.
